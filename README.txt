HA Accounting — Dashboard card colors and detail reports

Files: index.html and service-worker.js.

Dashboard summary cards now use distinct accent colors. Selecting a card opens its detailed report with print and Save PDF options. Sales and purchase reports include invoice items, totals, and balances. Other reports cover revenue, expenses, profit, today, cash, bank, inventory, customer debts, supplier dues, and direct-sale invoices.

The direct-sale report card opens its detail list; the quick action and market sidebar shortcut still open the direct-sale invoice form. This package retains the previous direct-sale and simple-home card improvements.

Install by replacing these files in the current project.
